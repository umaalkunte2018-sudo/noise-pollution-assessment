How to interpret results

Screening aid, not a legal compliance certificate
Within selected limit

The entered value is at or below the illustrative limit configured for that zone and period.

Near limit

The reading is within 5 dB of the configured limit. Review sampling conditions.

Above selected limit

The entered value exceeds the configured limit; confirm with repeat measurements.

Noise is often evaluated using equivalent continuous sound level, LAeq, over a defined period. A single spot reading is not equivalent to a full-period LAeq assessment.

Data, limitations & methodology

• Records are saved in your browser using local storage. They do not automatically sync across devices.

• Export CSV regularly as a backup. Clearing browser storage may erase your records.

• The dashboard uses manual values only. It does not measure sound by itself.

• If microphone functionality is added later, phone/laptop microphone levels are approximate unless calibrated and validated; they should not be treated as certified dB(A) readings.

• Use consistent sampling duration, microphone height, distance, weather notes, and meter settings. Note unusual events.

• For India, verify the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, applicable amendments, CPCB guidance, and local notifications before concluding compliance.
